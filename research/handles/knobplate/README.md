## Method

- **Scale: the leaf's HEIGHT at the plate's column over 2050 mm** — 1367 px,
  0.667 px/mm, so 1.5 mm a pixel. The leaf's width reads 0.353 of its height,
  narrower than a standard leaf, which is why the height is the scale.
- **Edge profiles, which held here**: a satin plate on a dark door, so the
  steepest step across each edge is sharp. Widths every 5 mm down the plate,
  the head and foot every 5 mm across, a circle fitted to the knob's outline.
- The head's lit rolled edge is part of the plate; the foot's rolled edge faces
  down, reads DARKER than the door, and ends at a dark contact line — the plate
  ends there, not where the bright face does (8 mm higher).
- Origin: the knob's spindle, y down. Mirrored, because the door is hinged right.

## Against d092

The gallery's bronze door with this fitting (`research/works/doors/d092.jpeg`)
is 3.8 mm a pixel (its leaf 543 px tall) and taken from the side. At that
resolution it reads **219 × 91, the knob 0.30 down the plate, the egg's centre
102 below the knob** — the same plate. `screenshots/lockset-knobplate.png`
makes d092's plate look taller and narrower than ours; that is the oblique view
and the tool's leaf scaling, not the fitting.

## Against the עילי

The same family — a waisted plate, domed head and foot, the same egg low down,
the same height to within 4 mm (216 against 220) and the same place for the
narrowest point (0.54). **But not the same plate**: the knob-plate's waist is
70 where the עילי's is 78, on photographs read the same way. So each keeps its
own outline (`knobPlateOutline`, `ilaiOutline`); what they share is the satin
plate itself (`satinPlate`), so their metal cannot drift apart.

## The camera

The knob stands ~55 mm proud. Its outline's centre reads 1 mm toward the
closing edge from the plate's centre line, where the full camera model the
Rotem's README describes would put it 5–9 mm. Another reading that the full
model overstates what these photographs show; one door, so it settles nothing.

## Not changed, on purpose

  matches on nickel. d092 is bronze.
- **The key slot's position** is not a reading (the film hides it). It sits
  where the Rotem's measured slot sits in its egg.
- **The price**, +₪200.
