## Method

- **Scale: each leaf's HEIGHT at the bow's column over 2050 mm**, read off
  ruled crops of the head and threshold: 1119 / 1068 / 1123 px, so **0.546 /
  0.521 / 0.548 px/mm**, about 1.9 mm a pixel.
- **Door 4 is shot from the side** (a strong horizontal perspective), so it
  gave only fractions ALONG the bar, and the form of the posts from the side.
- **Readings**: a threshold silhouette along door 2's black bow (the cleanest:
  black on white), edge profiles across each shaft, and door 1 for the rose
  and ball, where the copper ball stands out from its darker rose. mm from the
  bow's centre, then from its outboard tip.

## What the photographs show that the drawing had wrong

1. **The shaft was a third too thin**: 18.7 against 25.5.
2. **The ends were drawn from a few corpus pixels**: a 20 mm bead with a stub
   past it, and a flat ring. All four doors show a stem out of the post, a
   finial wider than the shaft (a double cone on door 2, a reel on door 1),
   a neck, and a small knob at the tip.
3. **It was 20 mm short**, and its rose 8 mm small.

The tone across the shaft (`grabRod`: a dark top line, a specular a quarter
down, a dark core, a bounce along the bottom) was already the photographs',
read the same way here, and did not move.

## What it cost

- **The trio's handle plate is 287 mm of flat field on the standard leaf**
  (Peretz's 0.23 inset), and the owner's son's 24.9 rule wants the bow WHOLLY
  inside it. The 300 bar stands 9.5 mm clear each side; the declared footprint
  (`in: GRAB.len + 10`) did not, by 0.5 mm, and the trio refused the bow on the
  standard and double sizes. The margin is for the drawing's strokes and the
  drawn metal ends at exactly 300, so it is `+ 5` now. The bar was not
  shortened to pass: 297 and 298 were measured.
- **The recessed channel, a Coral lever and the bow no longer go together**
  on the standard and double sizes (8 designs in the collide sweep, both lever
  styles that carry the Coral's blade). A centred 300 bar's tip is 10 mm
  nearer the lock than a 280 one's, and the channel between them and the
  lever's 133 mm blade no longer fit. Peretz's ranking decides it (the bow
  outranks the bar, the bar the lever): the lever yields to the cylinder. It is
  geometry, not a margin — with no outboard margin at all the 8 stay refused.
